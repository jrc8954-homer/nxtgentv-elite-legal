NxtGenTV Elite

Privacy Policy · Effective November 5, 2026

The short version: your data stays on your device. NxtGenTV Elite has no accounts of its own, no analytics service, no advertising, and no servers that receive your data. We cannot see what you watch, and we do not want to.

What the app stores, and where

Everything below is stored only on your device, in the app's own private storage:

  • IPTV service credentials you enter — server address, username, password, or portal/MAC details. The active login is encrypted using your device's hardware-backed keystore. Saved profiles are stored in the app's sandboxed storage. These credentials are sent only to the IPTV service you yourself configured, never to us or anyone else.
  • Viewing state — watch history, favorites, resume positions, recordings, scheduled recordings, and your settings.
  • Performance diagnostics — written only to a local file on your device to adapt playback to your hardware. They are never uploaded; the app contains no mechanism to do so.

Network connections the app makes

  • Your IPTV provider — to sign in, load channel and program listings, and stream the content you request. Your credentials and requests go directly to the provider you configured. That provider's own privacy practices apply to that connection.
  • The Movie Database (TMDB) and public image services — to fetch artwork and metadata for titles in your listings. These requests contain title lookups only, never your identity or credentials.
  • DNS resolution — if your network's DNS fails, the app may resolve addresses via encrypted DNS-over-HTTPS (Google Public DNS or Cloudflare). Only hostnames are sent, exactly as with any ordinary DNS lookup.

Microphone and voice

Voice commands use the microphone only while the voice panel is open and listening, and only after you allow it. The first time, the app explains this and asks for your permission; if you choose to type instead, the microphone stays off. You can turn the permission off for the app in your device's system settings at any time.

Your device's own speech service turns what you say into text. That service is part of your device, provided by Google or by the device maker, and on some devices it needs an internet connection; its own privacy terms apply. The app does not record or keep audio, and we receive none.

The text is matched to commands on your device. It leaves your device only when EliteAI is on and the built-in commands cannot handle it, as described next.

EliteAI

EliteAI is optional. It stays off until you add your own key from an AI provider you choose and agree to a notice that says what is sent. We do not supply, resell or pay for that service, and we receive none of what is sent to it.

When you use EliteAI, the app sends the following from your device straight to the provider you chose:

  • What you ask: the text you type, or the text the speech service heard (its first 500 characters), with whether you are on a TV or a phone and whether the player is open.
  • What the app looks up in your library to answer: titles, channel names, programme titles, short details such as plot, cast, director, year, genre, rating and length, episode titles, and your favorites and recordings lists when you ask about them.
  • Search boxes: when a search describes what you want (for example three or more words, or a genre, a year or an actor), the first 200 characters of the words you typed, to turn them into a filter.
  • Ask chips and picks: the details of the title you pick one for, or the programmes on now in the category you are in.
  • Guide matching, in the background and at most four times a day: the names, countries and channel numbers of channels that have no guide, and of the guide channels they might match.

The app never sends your TV service login, server address or playlist links, your watch history, or your key to anyone but your provider. Before anything is sent, the app replaces links, your provider's server details and login, email addresses, device (MAC) and network (IP) addresses and key-like strings with placeholders.

The provider's terms and privacy policy apply to what it receives. Some free plans let the provider read requests or use them to improve its products; the app shows each provider's notice before you agree.

Your key is stored only on your device, encrypted with the Android keystore, and is kept out of backups and device transfers. In Settings you can turn EliteAI off (the key stays saved) or remove the key at any time.

If you report an AI answer, the report is saved on your device first. It reaches us only if you send the email the app prepares, and then only what the email shows: the reason, the AI provider and model, the app version, the time and the answer, plus your question if you ticked that box.

What we never do

  • No collection, sale, or sharing of personal data by us. EliteAI requests go only to the AI provider you chose, as described above. No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No advertising. No account registration with us.
  • Cloud and device backups are disabled for the app's data, including all credentials.

Content

NxtGenTV Elite is a media player. It ships with no channels, no playlists, and no content sources. It plays only media from services you supply and are authorized to use. You are responsible for your own service subscriptions.

Children

NxtGenTV Elite is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, regardless of age.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or using Android's "Clear storage," removes everything the app stored. Nothing exists anywhere else to delete.
